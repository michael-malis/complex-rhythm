Mathematical notes

For nonzero z, the arguments of its n-th roots form n equally spaced phases. Changing arg(z) rotates them; changing |z| alone does not change event timing.

The four circle radii represent instrument lanes, not the moduli of the complex roots. A union merges coincident events: has six distinct phases, not seven.

A polyrhythm combines different periodicities across instruments. Composed root sets combine rhythms within one instrument. Both differ from an arbitrary step sequencer.
